2. SCOPE OF THIS POLICY

This policy applies to information collected through our official website (https://madebypsycho.github.io), direct email communications, and customer support inquiries. Our mobile applications maintain dedicated privacy policy pages tailored specifically to their device permissions and data access.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

When you contact us via email, submit an inquiry form, or participate in beta testing programs, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The content of your message, feedback, or support request.
  • Any diagnostic logs or hardware information you voluntarily provide to troubleshoot an issue.

B. Website Technical Data (Standard Server Logs)

When hosted on static platforms such as GitHub Pages, standard technical access logs (such as IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and request timestamps) may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver web pages securely and detect malicious traffic. Psycho Labs does not sell, broker, or build advertising profiles from these logs.

C. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website is designed as a lightweight, privacy-friendly static website. We do not use cookies, web beacons, tracking pixels, third-party marketing cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling scripts on this website. We only use browser localStorage solely to remember your preferred visual theme (Light Mode or Dark Mode) locally on your device.

4. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Any information we receive is used solely to:

  • Respond to your technical questions, bug reports, and project inquiries.
  • Maintain, test, and improve our software applications and website.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and developer program policies (including Google Play Developer Distribution Agreements).

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & DATA SHARING

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information under any circumstances.

We utilize trusted third-party service providers solely to operate our static infrastructure and deliver communications:

  • Static Web Hosting: GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) to host and serve our static website files securely via HTTPS.
  • Email & Form Delivery Services: Formspree (provided by Formspree, Inc.) to securely receive and forward contact form transmissions, and Google (Gmail) to receive and reply to user inquiries.

These providers process information strictly on our behalf to deliver their respective technical infrastructure services and adhere to industry-standard data security and privacy practices.

6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

We retain correspondence and support emails only as long as necessary to address your inquiry, provide ongoing software support, or satisfy legal and record-keeping requirements.

You may request the permanent deletion of your communication history with us at any time by sending an email to psycholabssupport@gmail.com. We will process verified requests within 30 days.

7. DATA SECURITY

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. Communications with our website are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website and applications are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

9. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, & GLOBAL USERS)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to access and receive a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to request the erasure of your personal data.
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at psycholabssupport@gmail.com.

10. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our software, legal requirements, or operational practices. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
