REVERSE VIEW

Android Video & Automotive Utility • UVC USB Bridge • v1.0.4

Project Overview

Reverse View is an Android utility engineered to turn a compatible Android smartphone or tablet into a dedicated, responsive rear-view camera monitor. By interfacing directly with USB video capture cards (UVC standard), Reverse View displays live video from any analog or composite vehicle backup camera with minimal latency.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Plug-and-Play USB Video Class (UVC): Interfaces directly with Android's USB Host API without requiring root privileges.
  • Automatic USB Auto-Launch: Detects when a rear camera USB capture device is attached or powered on and automatically brings the video feed to the foreground.
  • Floating Window & Lockscreen Display: Optional picture-in-picture overlay and lock screen reverse view capabilities.
  • Low-Latency Hardware Pipeline: Direct frame decoding optimized for real-time visual maneuvering and vehicle reversing safety.
  • Customizable Screen Overlays: Built-in parking guidelines, mirror flip (horizontal inversion), and full-screen aspect ratio scaling.
  • Hardware Diagnosis & Setup Guide: Integrated hardware testing suite and connection instructions for USB-OTG and capture cards.
  • Pro Upgrade Options: Flexible Monthly Subscription or Lifetime one-time unlock via Google Play In-App Billing to remove ads and unlock advanced continuous monitoring.
Official Release on Google Play:
Reverse View is officially launched and published on the Google Play Store! Supports Android devices (API 24+ / Android 7.0 to 14+) with USB Host support.

AUTO LAUNCHER

Android Auto & In-Vehicle Utility
🚗

Project Overview

Auto Launcher is an independent Android utility project designed to provide drivers with an uncluttered, distraction-free interface for their favorite automotive applications, diagnostics, and navigation tools.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • High-Visibility UI: Oversized touch targets, high-contrast typography, and intuitive swipe gestures tailored for in-vehicle environments.
  • Quick App Routing: Fast one-tap access to navigation, music playback, and communication tools.
  • Automatic Day/Night Contrast: Dynamic contrast shifting to ensure eye comfort under varying light and night-driving conditions.
  • Independent Utility Architecture: Designed as a standalone Android utility; no proprietary vehicle lock-in or telemetry sharing.
