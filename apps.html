REVERSEVIEW

Android Video & Camera Utility • UVC USB Bridge
📹

Project Overview

ReverseView is an Android utility engineered to turn a compatible Android smartphone or tablet into a responsive rear-view camera monitor. By interfacing directly with low-cost, widely available USB video capture cards (UVC standard), ReverseView displays live video from any analog or composite vehicle backup camera with minimal latency.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • Plug-and-Play USB Video Class (UVC): Interfaces directly with Android's USB Host API without requiring root privileges.
  • Low-Latency Video Pipeline: Direct hardware frame decoding for real-time visual feedback when reversing or maneuvering.
  • Customizable Screen Overlays: Optional parking guide lines, horizontal mirroring, and brightness adjustments.
  • Local-First Processing: Video frames are processed entirely in memory on-device and never saved, uploaded, or transmitted across a network.
Development & Store Status:
ReverseView is currently in active development and internal testing on various Android hardware devices. It has not yet been released on the Google Play Store. Public release announcements and testing invitations will be published here.

AUTO LAUNCHER

Android Auto & In-Vehicle Utility
🚗

Project Overview

Auto Launcher is an independent Android utility project designed to provide drivers with an uncluttered, distraction-free interface for their favorite automotive applications, diagnostics, and navigation tools.

Key Architectural Capabilities

  • High-Visibility UI: Oversized touch targets, high-contrast typography, and intuitive swipe gestures tailored for in-vehicle environments.
  • Quick App Routing: Fast one-tap access to navigation, music playback, and communication tools.
  • Automatic Day/Night Contrast: Dynamic contrast shifting to ensure eye comfort under varying light and night-driving conditions.
  • Independent Utility Architecture: Designed as a standalone Android utility; no proprietary vehicle lock-in or telemetry sharing.
Trademark & Affiliation Disclaimer:
Auto Launcher is an independent software project developed by Psycho Labs. It is not published by, sponsored by, or officially affiliated with Google LLC, Android, or Android Auto.
