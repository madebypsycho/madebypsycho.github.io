1. OVERVIEW & NATURE OF THE APP

Reverse View is an Android utility application designed to connect to an external USB Video Class (UVC) capture card, EasyCap adapter, or USB camera via USB-OTG and display a real-time, low-latency video feed on an Android smartphone or tablet screen (typically serving as an auxiliary vehicle rear-view monitor, backup camera display, or trailer camera).

Psycho Labs is committed to complete transparency regarding hardware permissions, on-device video rendering, advertising, and billing disclosures in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

2. DATA ACCESSED & HARDWARE PERMISSIONS

To deliver real-time external camera monitoring and automotive display features, Reverse View requests the following Android runtime and manifest permissions:

A. USB Host Permission (android.hardware.usb.host)

  • Purpose: When you connect a compatible USB capture card or camera via a USB-OTG adapter or Y-cable, the Android operating system prompts you to grant Reverse View access to the attached USB hardware device.
  • Usage: This permission is used strictly to establish low-latency communication with the UVC video peripheral and pull live video frames.
  • Storage & Transmission: USB communication occurs directly between the physical hardware and local device RAM. No peripheral serial numbers, device telemetry, or hardware identifiers are ever uploaded or transmitted across the internet.

B. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

  • Purpose: Android treats external USB video capture devices (UVC interfaces) under the system Camera subsystem. Runtime camera permission is required by the operating system to stream frames from external capture cards.
  • Usage: Reverse View accesses only the video stream provided by the connected external USB video device. It does not activate your smartphone's built-in front or rear cameras unless you manually select them.
  • Strict Local-Only Processing: Video frames are decoded and displayed in volatile memory in real time. Reverse View does NOT record, save, store, or transmit any video footage or camera frames to any remote server or third party.

C. Microphone / Audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Purpose: Certain USB capture cards deliver a combined video/audio stream over USB. If enabled, this allows you to monitor auxiliary audio lines from the camera or vehicle input.
  • Usage: Audio is processed strictly in real-time on-device. Reverse View does not record, save, or transmit your voice, vehicle audio, or background sound.

D. System Alert Window & Floating Overlay (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

  • Purpose: Reverse View includes an optional picture-in-picture floating overlay and lockscreen mode, allowing the reverse camera feed to appear automatically over other applications (e.g., GPS navigation) when reverse gear is triggered or when the USB device is attached.
  • Usage: Used strictly to draw the camera view over other apps while reversing. No screen content from other apps is read, recorded, or monitored.

E. Foreground Services (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICE)

  • Purpose: Enables continuous, low-latency video streaming in the foreground without the Android OS killing or freezing the video pipeline due to battery optimization during active vehicle maneuvering.
  • Notification (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): A persistent status notification is displayed while the camera service is active to ensure you are always aware that the USB camera pipeline is running.

F. Wake Lock (android.permission.WAKE_LOCK)

  • Purpose: Keeps the display awake and responsive while the camera stream is running so the screen does not dim or lock during parking maneuvers.

G. Network & Internet Access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

  • Purpose: Required exclusively to load Google AdMob advertisements in the free version of the application and to communicate with Google Play Billing to verify and restore Pro purchases. Video streaming itself operates 100% offline.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & ADVERTISING

A. Google AdMob (Advertising in Free Version)

The free edition of Reverse View displays banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

  • AdMob may collect and process anonymous device data, including your Google Advertising ID (GAID), IP address, app interaction telemetries, and coarse diagnostic statistics to serve non-intrusive ads and prevent fraud.
  • For more information on how Google processes advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.
  • Ad Removal: Users who upgrade to Reverse View Pro enjoy a completely ad-free experience, and AdMob ad requests are disabled.

B. Google Play In-App Billing (Pro Upgrades)

Reverse View offers optional premium features through Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC):

  • Reverse View Pro Monthly Subscription (reverseview_pro_monthly)
  • Reverse View Pro Lifetime License (reverseview_pro_lifetime)

All payment transactions, credit card processing, currency conversions, and billing validations are handled securely and exclusively by Google Play. Psycho Labs never receives, handles, or stores your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses.

C. Google Play Developer Console (Vitals & Crash Reporting)

We may receive aggregated, anonymous technical reports (such as ANRs and crash stack traces) provided automatically through the Google Play Developer Console. These reports contain no personal data or video frames and are used solely to debug device compatibility across different Android hardware models.

4. PERSONAL IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION (PII)

Reverse View does NOT collect, harvest, sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information (PII).

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or vehicle VIN number.
  • No user account, cloud registration, or social media login is required to use Reverse View.
  • We do not track, log, or record your physical GPS location or driving routes.

5. LOCAL DATA STORAGE & DELETION

All custom user configurations—including parking guideline positions, horizontal flip/mirror toggles, rotation angles, aspect ratio settings, and auto-start preferences—are stored locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences.

Because no user data or video footage is ever uploaded to remote servers, there is no remote account data to delete. You can permanently wipe all local application data at any time by clearing Reverse View's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

6. DATA SECURITY

We implement Android platform security standards:

  • External video feeds are processed within isolated Android application sandboxes.
  • No network sockets or external ports are opened to transmit camera frames.
  • All network communications for AdMob and Google Play Billing are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS encryption.

7. CRITICAL AUTOMOTIVE SAFETY DISCLAIMER

DRIVER RESPONSIBILITY NOTICE:
Reverse View is an auxiliary driver-assistance tool. It is not an autonomous safety system, blind-spot replacement, or certified collision avoidance device.
External USB video connections may be subject to physical cable disconnection, signal degradation, or electrical latency. Drivers must always visually inspect their vehicle's physical mirrors, conduct shoulder checks, and observe surrounding traffic and pedestrians before reversing or operating any motor vehicle.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Reverse View is an automotive utility application and is not intended for or directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect any data from children.
