REVERSEVIEW PRIVACY POLICY

Specific privacy disclosures for the ReverseView Android camera utility developed by Psycho Labs.

APPLICATION PRIVACY POLICY:
This policy applies specifically to the ReverseView Android application developed by Psycho Labs.

Application Name: ReverseView
Package Name: com.psycholabs.reverseview
Developer / Organization: Psycho Labs (Psycho Labs)
Developer Contact Email: psycholabssupport@gmail.com
Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. OVERVIEW & NATURE OF THE APP

ReverseView is an Android utility application designed to connect to an external USB Video Class (UVC) capture device or USB camera and render a live video stream on the device screen (typically used as an auxiliary rear-view monitor or backup camera).

Psycho Labs is committed to complete transparency regarding hardware access, on-device data processing, and user privacy.

2. DATA ACCESSED & HARDWARE PERMISSIONS

To perform its primary functionality, ReverseView requires access to specific device hardware APIs. Below is a complete breakdown of permissions requested and how each is utilized:

A. USB Host Permission (android.hardware.usb.host)

  • Purpose: When you connect a compatible USB capture card or camera via USB-OTG, the application requests standard Android system permission to communicate with the USB device.
  • Usage: This permission is used exclusively to negotiate USB video communication protocols (UVC standard) and pull video frames for display.
  • Storage & Transmission: USB communication occurs directly between the physical USB peripheral and local device memory. No peripheral identifiers or USB serial numbers are sent over the internet.

B. Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

  • Purpose: Depending on the Android OS version and underlying camera stack (e.g., Android Camera2 API or UVC direct drivers), Android may require runtime camera permission to read external video frames.
  • Usage: ReverseView accesses only the video feed of the connected external USB video device. It does not activate your device's built-in front or rear cameras unless you explicitly toggle to a device camera mode.
  • Real-Time Processing: Video frames are processed in volatile RAM in real time strictly for live rendering on your screen. ReverseView does NOT record, save, store, or transmit any video, audio, or photographic frames to any external server or third party.

C. Foreground Service (android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE)

  • Purpose: If enabled, used to keep the video display active and prevent the operating system from terminating the video pipeline while maneuvering or parking.

3. PERSONAL DATA COLLECTION

ReverseView does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require account registration, login credentials, location data, or contact lists to use the application.

4. DIAGNOSTICS & CRASH REPORTING

ReverseView does not include any third-party marketing SDKs, advertising trackers, or remote telemetry frameworks. The application functions entirely offline. Anonymous crash diagnostics may only be processed through standard Google Play Console developer vitals solely to identify and fix stability bugs without accessing camera feeds or personal data.

5. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & DATA SHARING

We do not sell, rent, license, or share any data from ReverseView with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

Third-party libraries utilized within the app are strictly limited to open-source video processing and Android compatibility libraries:

  • Standard Android Jetpack and open-source USB video class (UVC) capture libraries.

6. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Because ReverseView does not collect or store user data on remote servers, there is no remote user data to retain or delete. All user preferences (such as screen orientation, mirror mode, or guide-line toggles) are stored locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences and are permanently removed when the application is uninstalled or its local data is cleared.

7. DATA SECURITY

We adhere to Android security best practices. Video stream decoding is handled locally within the application sandbox. No external network connections are established to transmit camera feeds.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

ReverseView is a general automotive hardware utility. It is not directed toward children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

If we update ReverseView to introduce new features, hardware protocols, or diagnostic tools, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly with a revised "Last Updated" date.
