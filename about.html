STUDIO PROFILE INDEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT

A LITTLE DIFFERENT BY DESIGN.

Psycho Labs is an independent software studio focused on building useful, creative and thoughtfully designed digital products.

WHO WE ARE

Psycho Labs is an independent software and mobile app studio. We experiment with ideas, solve everyday problems and turn interesting concepts into practical applications.

In an industry often dominated by carbon-copy enterprise templates and bloated software, we deliberately take a different path. We love unconventional technical puzzles—connecting external video feeds to mobile screens, rethinking automotive interfaces, and building utilities that work reliably when you need them.

OUR ENGINEERING ETHOS

Our work is rooted in three straightforward principles:

  • Experiment Relentlessly: True innovation rarely happens inside safe committee meetings. We test edge cases, interface with quirky hardware standards (like USB Video Class and OTG bridges), and prototype quickly.
  • Keep Things Useful: Novelty without function is just noise. Every application we develop is built to solve a concrete task—whether that is parking a trailer safely with an auxiliary camera or simplifying dashboard controls on the road.
  • Make Technology Feel Better: We care deeply about touch latency, clean layouts, and software that respects user attention.

OUR FOCUS AREAS

1. Android Hardware & USB Video Solutions

Modern Android devices pack extraordinary processing power and high-resolution displays. Our flagship project, Reverse View, turns compatible Android handsets into dedicated, real-time monitors for external USB video capture devices, enabling accessible rear-view and trailer camera setups without requiring costly proprietary display hardware.

2. In-Car & Utility Interfaces

Automotive environments demand total focus and minimal distraction. Our Auto Launcher project explores streamlined app routing, large touch targets, and distraction-minimized mobile experiences for drivers.

TRANSPARENCY & PRIVACY

As an independent studio, our relationship with our users is our most valuable asset. We believe software should do what it says on the tin, without hidden tracking, invasive background telemetry, or unnecessary permission requests.

When our applications interact with hardware components—such as USB buses, cameras, or local file storage—they do so strictly on-device to perform the requested function.
